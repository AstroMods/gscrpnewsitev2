Garden StateFAQ

Quick answers before you join.

Questions

How do I connect to the server?

Press Connect on this page. Or open FiveM, press F8 and enter connect cfx.re/join/e66dkoa.

Do I need roleplay experience?

No. New players are welcome, and each department trains recruits before they patrol alone.

Can I play a civilian?

Yes. Civilian jobs, crews and storylines are a core part of the city.

What do I need to play?

A PC copy of Grand Theft Auto V, a working microphone and the Discord app.

How do I apply for a department?

Join the Discord and open the application for the department you want.
