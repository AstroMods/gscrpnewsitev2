Pick your side of the lights

Every unit has its own training, ranks and equipment. Choose one to see what it is like to serve.

San Andreas State Police

Statewide troopers covering the highways and back roads of San Andreas.

  • Highway patrol and traffic enforcement
  • Field training with experienced troopers
  • Arrest, report and warrant workflow

Blaine County Sheriff's Office

Deputies serving Blaine County, from Paleto Bay to the Grand Senora desert.

  • County-wide patrol and rural response
  • Working alongside state and municipal units
  • Deputy training and ranks

Municipal Police

City officers answering calls in the busiest streets of the urban core.

  • Neighborhood patrol and community calls
  • Quick response with dispatch support
  • Academy and field training

San Andreas Fire and EMS

Engines, rescue crews and medics who arrive when the worst day happens.

  • Structure fires, rescues and medical calls
  • Patient care reports after every call
  • Realistic injury and treatment system

FBI

Federal agents working major investigations with the DOJ and courthouse.

  • Long-running case work and warrants
  • Joint operations with local departments
  • Serious, story-driven roleplay
