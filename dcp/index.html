Dual Clan Policy

The rules for members, staff and leadership who are also part of other FiveM servers and communities, protecting our community from conflicts of interest, leaks and poaching.

Applies to all members, staff and leadership · Version 1.0 · Effective

Purpose & Scope

  1. This policy sets the rules for members, staff and leadership who also take part in other FiveM servers, communities, departments or organizations (“dual clanning”).
  2. Being active elsewhere is not wrong in itself. The aim is to protect the community’s members, staff, resources, internal information and reputation from conflicts of interest, leaks, poaching and drama.
  3. It applies to every member, staff member and leader, in every server, channel, voice call and platform run by or tied to the community, and to off-platform conduct that affects the community.
  4. No rank, tenure, friendship or reputation places anyone above this policy. Staff and leadership are held to a higher standard.
  5. This policy adds to the community’s general rules. If they conflict, the stricter standard applies.

Definitions

  1. Dual Clanning means being an active member, staff member, whitelisted player or role-holder in another FiveM server or community while a member of this one.
  2. Outside Community means any other FiveM server, roleplay community, discord, department, gang, company or organization outside this community.
  3. Competing Community means an Outside Community that targets the same player base or runs similar roleplay, and any community Management designates as competing.
  4. Sensitive Role means any staff, leadership, department command, developer, whitelisting or trusted position that gives access to non-public information or tools.
  5. Confidential Material means internal documents, staff channels, applications, reports, moderation logs, configs, scripts, assets, department structures and anything not publicly released.
  6. Conflict of Interest means any situation where outside involvement could reasonably influence, or appear to influence, a person’s duties, decisions or loyalty to this community.

Permitted Participation

  1. Members may play on other servers and join other communities, unless this policy or a specific Management ruling says otherwise.
  2. Membership in an Outside Community does not by itself lead to any penalty. Penalties come from the conduct and conflicts described in this policy.
  3. Members in Competing Communities may take part in this community’s normal public activity, but may not hold Sensitive Roles without written approval from Management.
  4. Members must keep their roles, characters, departments and reputations in each community separate, and must not use one to gain an unfair advantage in another.
  5. Members must not be pressured, shamed or harassed for choosing to play elsewhere, by staff or by other members.

Disclosure Requirements

  1. Staff and leadership must tell Management about every Outside Community they are a member, staff member or role-holder in, before taking on a Sensitive Role and again when anything changes.
  2. Members applying for departments, leadership, whitelisting or other trusted roles must disclose any outside roles in those same areas when asked.
  3. Disclosure must be honest and complete. Hiding, denying or lying about outside involvement is a more serious violation than the involvement itself.
  4. Management may ask for proof, such as screenshots or role listings, and may ask follow-up questions. Refusing to cooperate may be treated as non-disclosure.
  5. Disclosed information is kept confidential and used only to apply this policy.

Restricted & Prohibited Activity

  1. Sharing Confidential Material with anyone in an Outside Community is prohibited. This includes screenshots, recordings, summaries, paraphrases and verbal descriptions.
  2. Copying, reuploading, leaking or reselling this community’s scripts, assets, maps, configs, branding, department structures, SOPs or documents is prohibited.
  3. Using this community’s name, branding or reputation to promote, benefit or gain standing in an Outside Community is prohibited without approval.
  4. Joining or staying in an Outside Community in order to gather information, cause problems, steal members or sabotage anyone is prohibited.
  5. Recruiting, soliciting or pressuring members, staff or leadership to leave for an Outside Community is prohibited, including through private messages.
  6. Bringing drama, grudges or disputes from an Outside Community into this one, or the reverse, is prohibited.
  7. Using an alternate account, another identity or a third party to evade this policy, a ban or a restriction is prohibited.
  8. Encouraging, helping or covering for anyone who breaks this policy is a violation in itself.

Staff & Leadership Restrictions

  1. Staff and leadership must put the interests of this community first when acting in an official capacity, and must not use their position for their benefit or for an Outside Community’s.
  2. Staff and leadership may not hold staff, leadership, department command or other Sensitive Roles in a Competing Community without written approval from Management.
  3. Where outside involvement could create a Conflict of Interest, staff must disclose it and recuse themselves from related reports, rulings, applications, punishments, appeals and decisions.
  4. Staff must stay neutral and apply the same rules to every member, regardless of which communities they belong to or whether they are friends with them.
  5. Staff may not give preferential treatment, approvals, rewards, roles or leniency to members of their other communities or to themselves.
  6. Staff may not use staff permissions, logs, tools or information to benefit an Outside Community or to monitor, expose or target its members.
  7. Staff must never take Confidential Material, staff-only discussions or ongoing investigations into an Outside Community.
  8. Leaving the staff team does not end confidentiality obligations. Former staff may not share or use Confidential Material.
  9. Misconduct by staff or leadership under this section is treated more seriously than the same behavior by a regular member.
Higher standard: staff misconduct under this section may lead to removal from the staff team in addition to any other penalty.

Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

  1. Everything created for, funded by or developed within this community, including custom scripts, vehicles, maps, branding, tools and documentation, remains the community’s property unless Management has stated otherwise.
  2. Developers and contributors may not reuse, redistribute or port community assets to an Outside Community without written permission.
  3. Anyone who learns they have been sent or given Confidential Material by mistake must delete it and report it to staff.
  4. Members must not impersonate the community, its staff or its leadership in an Outside Community.
  5. Reports, applications, appeals and staff discussions are private. Posting or leaking them anywhere is a serious violation.

Cross-Server Conduct & Representation

  1. Your conduct in an Outside Community can affect this community’s reputation when you are known as one of our members. Hate speech, harassment, cheating, scamming, doxxing and similar conduct can lead to action here.
  2. Members must not speak for this community in Outside Communities unless they have been authorized to do so.
  3. Members must not trash-talk, mock or target this community, its staff or its members, or any Outside Community and its members, in public or in private.
  4. Do not escalate cross-server disputes by raiding, mass-reporting, brigading, spamming or harassing anyone. Take disputes to staff.
  5. Members banned from an Outside Community for serious misconduct, such as harassment, cheating, exploiting, threats or leaking, must tell staff if they hold a Sensitive Role. Management may review the case and take action here.

Recruitment & Advertising

  1. Advertising an Outside Community is allowed only in the channels and under the conditions Management designates, if any. Everywhere else it is prohibited.
  2. Advertisements must be truthful, and must not insult, compare unfavorably to, or disparage this community or any other.
  3. Mass messaging, spam, unsolicited direct messages and repeated posts are prohibited.
  4. Poaching is prohibited. Offering roles, ranks, rewards or special treatment elsewhere to draw people away from this community is a violation.
  5. Staff may not recruit for, or advertise, an Outside Community in any channel or tool they have access to as staff.
  6. Partnerships, cross-server events or collaborations require prior approval from Management.

Consequences & Disciplinary Action

  1. Violations may result in one or more of the actions below. Penalties need not be applied in order and may start at any level based on severity.
  2. Factors considered include severity, intent, harm caused, history, honesty and cooperation, whether a Sensitive Role was held, and whether Confidential Material was exposed.
  3. Aggravating factors include non-disclosure, lying, repeat offenses, use of alternate accounts, and retaliation against a reporter.

Reporting & Appeals

  1. Anyone may report a suspected violation to staff or Community Management with as much evidence as possible, such as screenshots, clips, logs and timestamps.
  2. Reports about staff or leadership may go directly to a higher authority, or to Community Management if the person involved is in leadership.
  3. Reports are reviewed confidentially and without bias. Good-faith reporters will not be punished, even if the report is not upheld.
  4. Knowingly false or malicious reports are a violation of this policy.
  5. A penalty may be appealed through the community’s appeals process with an explanation and any new evidence. Appeals are reviewed by someone not involved in the original decision.
  6. Penalties remain in effect during an appeal unless Community Management decides otherwise.

Management Discretion

  1. Community Management and Leadership may review any situation individually, including those not specifically described here, and take whatever action they consider appropriate to protect the community.
  2. Management may approve exceptions in writing, set conditions on outside involvement, require disclosure, or restrict access to Sensitive Roles while a matter is reviewed.
  3. Leadership may act on conduct that violates the spirit of a rule even when the wording does not cover it, and may take immediate temporary action during an investigation.
  4. Decisions aim to be fair, proportionate and consistent, and reasons will be shared with those affected where appropriate.
  5. Where Community Management itself has a conflict of interest, an uninvolved member of leadership will handle the matter.
  6. Leadership’s decision on a matter is final once any appeal has been completed.

Amendments & Acknowledgment

  1. Community Management may update this policy at any time. Significant changes will be announced, and continued participation means acceptance of the current version.
  2. Staff and leadership must re-read this policy after significant changes.
  3. Not having read this policy is not a defense for violating it.
