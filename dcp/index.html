Duel Clan Policy

The standards for forming, joining, leading and representing duel clans, and for keeping competition fair for everyone in our community.

Applies to all members, staff and leadership · Version 1.0 · Effective

Purpose & Scope

  1. This policy exists so duel clans add to the community’s competitive spirit without harming its culture, fairness or reputation.
  2. It applies to every member, staff member and leader of the community, on every server, channel, platform and event run by or associated with it.
  3. It covers conduct inside the community and off-platform conduct that targets, or is directed at, community members or clans.
  4. No clan, rank, title, friendship or reputation places anyone above this policy. Staff and leadership are held to a higher standard, not a lower one.
  5. This policy supplements the community’s general rules and terms of service. If the two conflict, the stricter standard applies.

Definitions

Duel Clan: an organized group of members who compete together or against others in duels, tournaments or related competitive activity, and who identify themselves with a shared name, tag, logo or banner.
  1. Clan Leader means the person with final authority over a clan’s roster, conduct and representation. Clan Officer means anyone the leader formally delegates authority to.
  2. Clan Member means anyone on a clan’s approved roster or openly displaying its tag or identity.
  3. Staff means moderators, administrators, event hosts and anyone holding a trusted role. Leadership means Community Management, owners and directors.
  4. Rival Clan means any clan a member’s clan competes against, disputes with or is publicly compared to.
  5. Targeting means repeated or coordinated actions aimed at a specific person or clan that serve to harass, exclude, intimidate or gain an unfair advantage.
  6. Conflict of Interest means any situation where a person’s clan, friendships, rivalries or personal gain could reasonably influence, or appear to influence, a decision.

Creating, Joining & Representing a Clan

  1. A new clan must be registered with Community Management before it uses the community’s name, channels, events or recruitment space. Registration includes the clan name, tag, leader and initial roster.
  2. Names, tags and logos must not be offensive, discriminatory, sexual, impersonate another clan, staff or the community, or mock a real person or group.
  3. Only the leader or a designated officer may speak officially for a clan. Opinions of individual members are their own unless the leader confirms otherwise.
  4. Members may belong to one active competitive clan at a time unless Community Management approves an exception in writing.
  5. Joining a clan is voluntary. No member may be pressured, tricked, bribed or threatened into joining, staying or leaving.
  6. Members who leave a clan must be allowed to do so without retaliation, public shaming or interference with their standing in the community.
  7. Clans must keep their roster current and notify Community Management of leadership changes, disbandment or mergers within a reasonable time.
  8. Anyone wearing a clan tag or speaking as a clan member represents that clan and the community, and is accountable for how that reflects on both.

Membership & Participation

  1. All clan members must follow the community rules in full. Clan membership is never a defense for a rule violation.
  2. Clans may set their own activity or skill standards, but these must be applied consistently and must not discriminate on protected characteristics.
  3. Clans may not exclude members from public community spaces, events or open duels because of clan affiliation or rivalry.
  4. Clans must not operate as closed cliques that harass outsiders or discourage non-members from participating.
  5. Inactive or disbanded clans must stop using the community’s name, branding and channels.
  6. Members are responsible for the conduct of any guests or recruits they bring into the community.

Respectful & Fair Competition

  1. All duels and clan events must be played honestly, within the posted rules, and in the spirit of good sportsmanship.
  2. Cheating, exploiting bugs, using unauthorized tools, match-fixing, win-trading, stat padding and intentionally throwing matches are prohibited.
  3. Duel results must be reported honestly. Falsifying, withholding or editing evidence of results is a serious violation.
  4. Agreed duel conditions, such as rules, wagers, loadouts and time limits, must be honored by all sides.
  5. Competitors must accept outcomes respectfully. Excessive taunting, gloating, rage-quitting, or disputing results by harassment is not permitted.
  6. Clans may not collude to block other clans from fair access to events, brackets, rankings or rewards.
  7. Winning does not excuse poor conduct, and losing does not excuse retaliation.

Rival Clans: Harassment, Toxicity & Targeting

  1. Rivalry must stay competitive and never become personal. Friendly trash talk is allowed only while all parties are clearly comfortable with it.
  2. Harassment, threats, slurs, hate speech, doxxing, stalking, impersonation and encouraging others to do any of these are prohibited, in public or in private messages.
  3. Clans and members may not brigade, mass-report in bad faith, spam, or coordinate pile-ons against another clan or individual.
  4. Members may not follow, ambush, grief or repeatedly engage a person or clan outside of agreed or organized competition in order to harass them.
  5. Rumors, edited screenshots, false accusations and “callout” posts about rival clans or members are not allowed. Disputes go through the reporting process, not public campaigns.
  6. Personal conflicts between members must not be dragged into clan or community spaces. Leaders must de-escalate and report ongoing issues to staff.
  7. Clan leaders are responsible for correcting the behavior of their members and must not encourage, reward or ignore harassment.
  8. Retaliating against anyone who reports a violation or acts as a witness is strictly prohibited and is treated as an aggravating factor.

Recruitment & Advertising

  1. Recruitment and clan advertising are allowed only in the channels or areas designated for them.
  2. Advertisements must be truthful and may not make false promises about rank, rewards, staff connections or special treatment.
  3. Unsolicited direct messages, mass pings, spam and repeated posting are prohibited. A clan may reasonably repost only after the cooldown set by staff.
  4. Poaching is not allowed. Clans may not pressure, bribe or harass members of other clans to switch, or lure them with misleading offers.
  5. Clans may not disparage other clans in their recruitment material, or compare themselves to rivals by name.
  6. Recruiters must respect the community’s age and conduct requirements and must not recruit anyone who has said no.
  7. Advertising for outside servers, communities or paid services requires prior approval from Community Management.
  8. Staff may not use their position, access or reputation to recruit for their own clan or promote it above others.

Clan Leadership Responsibilities

  1. Clan leaders are role models and are accountable for their clan’s conduct, including that of officers and recruits.
  2. Leaders must make this policy known to every member and ensure it is followed.
  3. Leaders must report serious violations they learn of, whether by their own members or by others, to staff in good faith.
  4. Leaders must cooperate with reviews, investigations and event administration, and must not obstruct, mislead or intimidate those carrying them out.
  5. Leaders may remove members from their clan for violations, but may not use removal to punish people for reporting issues or for criticizing leadership.
  6. Leaders must be transparent about clan rules, dues, requirements and promises made to members.
  7. A leader who steps down must hand over or disband the clan in an orderly way and must not take down or misuse shared community resources.

Staff Conduct, Conflicts of Interest & Neutrality

  1. Staff may be clan members, but their role in the community always takes priority. Staff must disclose their clan affiliation to Community Management.
  2. Staff may not use permissions, tools, information or influence to benefit themselves, their friends or their clan, or to disadvantage a rival.
  3. Staff may not grant their own clan special treatment, including roles, channels, event slots, rewards, rulings, scheduling or leniency.
  4. Staff must recuse themselves from any report, dispute, ruling, event or punishment involving their own clan, a rival clan, a personal friend or any case where they have a conflict of interest. They must hand it to an uninvolved staff member.
  5. Staff acting on a clan-related matter must stay neutral, impartial and consistent, and apply the same standard to every clan.
  6. Staff may not host, referee, judge or manage duels, tournaments or rankings in which their own clan is competing.
  7. Staff may not see, copy or share confidential reports, logs or moderation information with their clan or with anyone who has no need for it.
  8. Staff must not wear clan tags, make clan statements or involve themselves in clan disputes while acting in an official capacity.
  9. Staff must not delete, alter or conceal evidence, or suppress reports, involving themselves or their clan.
  10. Staff who see a possible conflict of interest, or the appearance of one, must disclose it immediately. Failure to disclose is itself a violation.
Higher standard: staff misconduct under this section is treated more seriously than the same behavior by a regular member, and may result in removal from the staff team in addition to other penalties.

Consequences & Disciplinary Action

  1. Violations may result in one or more of the actions below. Penalties need not be applied in order, and may start at any level based on severity.
  2. Factors considered include severity, intent, harm caused, history, whether the person cooperated, whether they held a position of trust, and whether others were pressured or involved.
  3. Aggravating factors include harassment of a reporter, dishonesty, repeat offenses, coordinated behavior, and abuse of staff or leadership authority.

Reporting & Appeals

  1. Anyone may report a suspected violation to staff or Community Management with as much evidence as possible, such as screenshots, clips, logs and timestamps.
  2. Reports about staff or leadership may be sent directly to a higher authority, or to Community Management if the person involved is in leadership.
  3. Reports are reviewed confidentially and without bias. Reporters will not be punished for good-faith reports, even if the report is not upheld.
  4. Knowingly false or malicious reports are a violation of this policy.
  5. A person who receives a penalty may appeal through the community’s appeals process, by explaining what happened and providing new evidence. Appeals are reviewed by someone not involved in the original decision.
  6. Penalties remain in effect while an appeal is pending unless Community Management decides otherwise.

Management Discretion

  1. Community Management and Leadership may review any situation individually, including those not specifically described in this policy, and take whatever action they consider appropriate to protect the community.
  2. Action may include warnings, restrictions, clan or staff sanctions, mediation, mandated cooling-off periods, event or roster changes, or disbanding a clan.
  3. Leadership may act on conduct that follows the spirit of a rule even when the wording does not cover it, and may take immediate temporary action while an investigation is ongoing.
  4. Decisions will aim to be fair, proportionate and consistent, and the reasons will be shared with those affected where it is appropriate to do so.
  5. Where Community Management itself has a conflict of interest, the matter will be handled by uninvolved leadership.
  6. Leadership’s decision on a matter is final once any appeal has been completed.

Amendments & Acknowledgment

  1. Community Management may update this policy at any time. Significant changes will be announced, and continued participation means acceptance of the current version.
  2. Clan leaders and staff must re-read this policy after significant changes.
  3. Not having read this policy is not a defense for violating it.
